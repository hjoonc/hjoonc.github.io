## ASPICE
Automotive SPICE (Automotive Software Process Improvement and Capability dEtermination)

## Process capability is determined using a two-dimensional framework.

- Process Dimension (PRM)
  - Processes defined in a Process Reference Model.
  - What the process is and what it is expected to achieve. (Purpose, Outcome)

- Capability dimension (PA & CL)
  - Capability levels subdived into process attributes.
  - Process attributes provide measurable characteristics of process capability.
  - How capable the process is (PA Rating: N/P/L/F → CL)

## PAM (PRM + Indicators → PA & CL)
- The Process Assessment Model selects processes from the PRM and supplements them with indicators.
- Indicators support the collection of objective evidence for process assessment and rating.
- A model that adds Indicators to the PRM to assess process capability based on objective evidence.
