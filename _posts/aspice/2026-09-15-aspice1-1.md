## PRM (Process Reference Model)
Processes are organized hierarchically as :

**1\) Process Category -> 2\) Process Group -> 3\) Process**

![Process Reference Model](/assets/img/posts/aspice/pic1-1-1-prm.png)

<br>

## 1) Process Category
There are **3 Process Categories**:
- **Primary Life Cycle Processes** :
  
&emsp;&emsp;Processes focused on product acquisition, supply, specification, design, development, integration, and testing.


- **Organizational Life Cycle Processes** :

&emsp;&emsp;Processes focused on developing process, product, and resource assets to achieve organizational business goals.


- **Supporting Life Cycle Processes** :

&emsp;&emsp;Processes that support other processes throughout the life cycle.

<br>

## 2) Process Group
There are **8 Process Groups** across the 3 Process Categories :
- Primary Life Cycle Processes :
  - **ACQ(Acquisition)**: Product/service acquisition (Customer)
  - **SPL(Supply)**: Product/service supply (Supplier)
  - **SYS(System)**: System development and testing
  - **SWE(Software Engineering)**: Software development and testing
    
- Organizational Life Cycle Processes :
  - **MAN(Management)**: Project/process management
  - **PIM(Process Improvement)**: Process improvement
  - **REU(Reuse)**: Reuse management
    
- Supporting Life Cycle Processes :
  - **SUP(Supporting)**: Life cycle process support

<br>

## 3) Processes
- Each Process Group consists of individual **Processes**.
- Each process is defined by:
  - **Purpose**: The unique functional objectives of the process.
  - **Outcomes**: The expected positive results achieved by performing the process.

<br>

The following chapters will focus only on the **processes in the SYS and SWE Process Groups**.
