## MF(Measurement Framework)
The Measurement Framework (MF) defines how process attributes are rated <br>
and used to determine a process’s capability level.

## Capability Level (CL)
A capability level represents a **process’s maturity** <br>
**based on a set of process attributes**, providing a structured path for process improvement.

![CL](/assets/img/posts/aspice/pic2-1-1-cl.png)

## 9 Process Attributes (PAs)
Within this process assessment model, <br>
the determination of capability is based upon the **nine process attributes** defined in ISO/IEC 33020. 

![PA](/assets/img/posts/aspice/pic2-1-2-pa.png)

## Process Attribute Rating
The ISO/IEC 33020 measurement framework provides <br> 
rating scales, methods, and aggregation methods for assessing process attributes.
